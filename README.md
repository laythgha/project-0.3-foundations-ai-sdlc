# Project 0.3: Foundations, AI-Driven SDLC

> **Confidential. Covered by your NDA.** Do not share this brief, copy it into a public repository, or describe what TSS assigns. The work you write is yours; the brief is not.

Palmstone Living, a small online homewares store, runs on six messy Google Sheets, and its managers cannot see the business in one place in time to make decisions. You take their problem through the whole Software Development Lifecycle in one week: discovery and requirements, planning, design, build, testing, deployment and maintenance. You finish with a dashboard that brings their data together.

First you learn each stage without AI, so you know what the work actually is. Then you run every stage AI-first, and you record what the AI did and what you decided yourself. You finish by walking through the whole journey in a Loom video.

The habit this project builds: before any task, ask "how can AI do this, how do I set it up to do it well, and what do I need to check?"
